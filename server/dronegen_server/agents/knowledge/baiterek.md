# Байтерек (Baiterek)

Description: the Baiterek tower in Astana, a 97 m tall white monument shaped like a tree of
life holding a golden egg: a slender white column rises from a wide base and at the top splits
into many white lattice branches that curve outward and upward like a tulip or a tree crown,
cradling a huge golden glass sphere (about a quarter of the tower's height in diameter). The base
also opens into white curved struts, like roots. Modern architecture, white steel and gold glass.

Must be right: one golden sphere held at the very top inside the white branches; the long slender
trunk (the sphere sits high, the tower is about 4-5 times taller than the sphere is wide);
white lattice, not solid.

Common mistakes: a ball on a plain pole; a TV tower; a tree with leaves; several spheres.

For 3D: a render in front view, or a view sheet. Symmetric around its axis.
