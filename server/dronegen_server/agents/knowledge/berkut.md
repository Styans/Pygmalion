# Беркут (golden eagle)

Description: a golden eagle (berkut), the hunting eagle of Kazakh falconers (berkutshi): a large
dark-brown eagle with golden-brown feathers on the back of the head and neck, a strong hooked
yellow-and-black beak, legs feathered down to the toes, big yellow talons, long broad wings with
separated "finger" feathers at the tips, and a fan-shaped tail. Spread wings are about 2.5 times
the body length.

Must be right: two wings with fingered tips; a hooked beak; feathered legs; dark brown plumage
with the golden nape.

Common mistakes: a bald eagle (white head); a hawk or falcon (thin, small); too many feathers in
noisy detail; extra legs.

For 3D: front view with the wings fully spread (the classic show silhouette): render style,
left-right symmetric. Or a side view of a perched eagle.
