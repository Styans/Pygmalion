# Домбра (dombra)

Description: a Kazakh dombra, a long-necked plucked lute with exactly two strings. A teardrop
(pear-shaped) wooden body with a thin flat soundboard and a deep rounded back made of 7-11 glued
wooden staves; a small soundhole in the soundboard; a small wooden bridge; the strings start at
a small button at the bottom end of the body. A long slender neck, about as long as the body or
longer, with thin frets tied around it. A flat head that continues the neck and tilts slightly
back, with exactly two wooden tuning pegs inserted from the back of the head. Natural honey or
brown wood, often with a thin Kazakh ornament inlay along the edge of the soundboard.

Must be right: exactly 2 strings; exactly 2 tuning pegs; teardrop body; long thin neck (the
whole instrument is about 1 m long, the body is about a third of it); flat soundboard.

Common mistakes of image models: a Chinese pipa or a mandolin instead (4 pegs, 4 strings, short
neck, frets glued on the soundboard); a round guitar-like soundhole with a rosette; a banjo-like
round body; pegs sticking out sideways like a violin scroll.

For 3D: a view sheet (front, side, back): the side view shows the body depth (0.3-0.5 of its
width) and the rounded back. Left-right symmetric.
