# Золотой человек (Altyn Adam)

Description: the Golden Man, a Saka warrior of the 4th century BC from the Issyk burial mound, a
national symbol of Kazakhstan (he stands on a winged snow leopard on the Independence monument in
Almaty). A standing young warrior seen from the front in a ceremonial costume covered with
thousands of small golden plates: a very tall pointed conical golden headdress (as tall as the
head and neck together) decorated in front with upright golden arrows, mountain-goat (argali)
figures and winged horses; a red caftan covered with small golden scale-like plates; a wide
golden belt with plaques; high boots; a short sword (akinak) at the right hip and a dagger at
the left; a golden torque around the neck.

Must be right: the tall pointed headdress with arrows; golden scales on the clothes; the belt
with weapons; a frontal, calm pose.

Common mistakes: a Roman or Greek helmet; a crown; a knight in plate armour; a pharaoh.

For 3D: a view sheet or a front render; left-right symmetric apart from the weapons.
