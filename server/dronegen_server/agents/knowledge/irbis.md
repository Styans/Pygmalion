# Ирбис (snow leopard)

Description: a snow leopard (irbis), the symbol of Almaty: a large, stocky mountain cat with thick
pale grey to cream fur covered with dark grey open rosettes and spots, a very long thick fluffy
tail (almost as long as the body), small rounded ears, a short muzzle, broad heavy paws, pale
grey-green eyes. Often shown leaping between rocks or crouching. On the arms of Almaty the snow
leopard has wings.

Must be right: the very long thick tail; pale fur with rosettes (not yellow with black spots);
four legs; small round ears.

Common mistakes: a yellow leopard or a cheetah; a tiger with stripes; a thin short tail; a house
cat.

For 3D: side view (leaping, the tail curved up behind), render style.
