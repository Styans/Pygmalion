# Кобыз (qyl-qobyz)

Description: a Kazakh qyl-qobyz, an ancient bowed string instrument about 70-90 cm long, held
upright. A carved wooden body shaped like a deep ladle or bucket: its upper part is open (a
hollow bowl with no top), its lower part is covered with a stretched leather skin; a short thick
neck without frets and without a fingerboard; a head bent slightly back with two tuning pegs.
Exactly two thick strings of twisted horsehair, played with an arched bow strung with
horsehair. Dark wood; old instruments carry small metal plates, little mirrors and pendants
(it was the instrument of the shaman-bard Korkyt).

Must be right: exactly 2 horsehair strings; the open upper half of the body; the leather-covered
lower half; a bow next to it if a player or a still life is shown.

Common mistakes: a violin or a cello (4 strings, closed body, f-holes, scroll head); a dombra
(plucked, frets); a guitar-like body.

For 3D: a view sheet; the side view shows the deep open bowl. Left-right symmetric.
