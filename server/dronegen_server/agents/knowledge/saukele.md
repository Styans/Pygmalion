# Саукеле (saukele)

Description: the saukele, the tall wedding headdress of a Kazakh bride: a tall narrow cone, about
as tall as a forearm, covered with red or white velvet and richly decorated with silver and gold
plates, pearls, coral and gemstones; a wide ornamented band around the base; long decorated
pendants (zhaktau) hanging down on both sides of the face; a tuft of white owl feathers (uki) on
top of the point; often a light veil falling from the back.

Must be right: one tall cone (not a crown); the two long side pendants; the feather tuft on top.

Common mistakes: a European princess hennin with a long veil only; a crown; a short round hat;
a turban.

For 3D: a view sheet or a front render; left-right symmetric.
