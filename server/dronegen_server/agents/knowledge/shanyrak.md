# Шанырак (shanyrak)

Description: the shanyrak, the round wooden crown at the top of a Kazakh yurt: a thick wooden
ring with bent wooden laths crossing inside it - two groups of three parallel arched laths that
cross each other at right angles and bulge upward into a low dome. Around the ring, many thin
straight roof poles (uyk) radiate outward like sun rays. Warm brown wood, often with red and
gold painted ornament on the ring. On the state emblem of Kazakhstan it is drawn from below,
golden, with the poles as rays on a sky-blue disk.

Must be right: a perfect circle; 3 + 3 crossing laths (a lattice, not a cross or a star);
radial poles all around.

Common mistakes: a wagon wheel with spokes from the centre; a Celtic cross; a star or a
snowflake; a flat ring without the crossing laths.

For 3D: flat style (top view) for a show emblem, or a render seen from below at an angle.
Symmetric in every direction.
