# Тулпар (tulpar)

Description: Tulpar, the winged horse of Kazakh and Turkic legend: a slim, strong steppe horse
with a flowing mane and tail and two large feathered wings rising from its shoulders, shown
galloping or leaping. On the state emblem of Kazakhstan two golden tulpars flank the shanyrak.

Must be right: one horse; exactly two wings, attached at the shoulders; four legs; a horse head
(not a dragon, not a griffin).

Common mistakes: a unicorn horn added by default; six legs or extra wings; wings growing from the
back of the saddle area; a western pegasus with a rider.

For 3D: side view (the profile shows the gallop and both wings raised), render style. Not
left-right symmetric in side view.
