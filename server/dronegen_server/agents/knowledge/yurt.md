# Юрта (kiiz uy)

Description: a Kazakh yurt (kiiz uy), a round felt dwelling: low cylindrical walls of a wooden
lattice (kerege) covered with white felt, a gently domed roof of curved poles (uyk) meeting at
the round shanyrak crown at the top, which is left open as a smoke hole. Wide bands of red, blue
and gold Kazakh ornament around the walls and along the roof edge, ropes tied around the felt, a
carved and painted wooden double door. The height is about half of the diameter.

Must be right: round plan; walls lower than the roof is wide; dome-like (not pointed) roof with
the round opening at the top; white felt.

Common mistakes: a tipi or a cone tent; a Mongolian ger with a flat roof only; a pointed
Chinese-style roof; square walls.

For 3D: a render in three-quarter view, or a view sheet. Rotationally symmetric apart from the
door.
